# V1-to-V2 Solidity review ledger

_Post-Kiyotaki-Wright vertical-slice review, 2026-08. This is a disposition ledger, not authority to rewrite the listed contracts._

## Boundary

The amended `AcceptanceThreshold.sol` proves that a narrow V2 contact point can remain Solidity-native while preserving a V1 comparison trace. It does not justify putting the full source model into one contract. Its unoptimised runtime is already 23,492 bytes against the 24,576-byte EIP-170 limit. Every later slice must therefore compose through small typed interfaces and events rather than enlarging Kiyotaki-Wright.

Status terms mean:

- **retain**: preserve a narrowly stated executable claim;
- **rewrite**: replace unsafe state or transitions under a new source card;
- **compose**: keep the mechanism as a separate optional module/profile;
- **evidence-only**: emit bounded observations but never advance another lifecycle directly;
- **proof/simulation only**: do not deploy the model as institutional runtime; and
- **retire**: preserve V1 only as a versioned counterexample or compatibility trace.

## Next vertical slice

The completed path now reaches scoped use, posting, explicit residual/discharge acknowledgement, contextual memory, bounded contest, typed observation aggregation and a generic indexed-obligation calendar. The ledger therefore selects **Schumpeter's credit-allocation profile** as the next slice.

Reason: the obligation mechanism now makes bilateral future terms, due dates, partial acknowledged performance, arrears, cure, forgiveness and termination explicit. Schumpeter is the next place the present claims resources from hoped-for future surplus, but V1 calls the first acceptable funder an allocation mechanism, relies on one uncontested score, races directly into default and assumes the interest source. The next plan must preserve Schumpeter's trace and decide the smallest composition of competing offers, bilateral selection, contested underwriting evidence, obligation reference and a separately contestable default/cure path. It must not infer productiveness, future revenue, borrower sincerity, fair price, insolvency or enforcement from a loan record.

## Result

The cast does not collapse into one super-contract. Solidity remains appropriate for bounded commitments, permissions, accounting invariants, clocks and lifecycle transitions. Simulation remains appropriate for propagation and heterogeneous behavioural response. Proof remains appropriate for arithmetic and state-machine properties. Delivery, desire, consent, truth, legitimacy and continued social cooperation remain explicit world boundaries.
