Fractional Trade Lab

Exchange first saves time through comparative advantage. Then repeated exchange compounds the gain by making people better at the task they specialise in. Money enters when that exchange has to scale beyond one matched pair.

Adam alone Spear: 4 hours
Axe: 3 hours
Both tools: 7 hours
Oz alone Spear: 1 hour
Axe: 2 hours
Both tools: 3 hours
Specialise and trade Adam makes two axes: 6 hours
Oz makes two spears: 2 hours
Two complete sets: 8 hours, not 10. Each saves one hour.
Prosperity loop Saved time becomes practice, tools, search, rest, or invention. The more they exchange, the more specialised they become.
Where money enters Bilateral trade still needs Adam and Oz to match. Fractional money lets each sell to anyone and carry a transferable claim.
Ye tally rope Adam gives an axe now. Oz ties a spear-knot on the shared rope. The rope is symmetric memory: claim, ruler, and settlement before full money.
