The contestability test

A large fee is not yet a verdict.

Coordination costs money. Extraction pressure rises when a central claim is opaque, asymmetric, difficult to refuse and impossible to challenge. Change the power conditions without changing the order.

Make the surface contestable

These switches alter power, not the €33.19 order.

The pressure index is a teaching device, not an empirical finding. Its point is the direction: transparency, exit, voice and recourse make a central claim easier to contest.

The boundary

Expense and extraction are not synonyms.

A platform may create real value and incur real costs. BOVI's X-ray asks what part of its claim is protected by informational advantage, concentrated control, weak exit and absent recourse. The point is not to pronounce every margin immoral. It is to make the power underneath a price visible enough to contest.

Ask of any exchange

  1. Who controls the junction?
  2. What leaves each person besides money?
  3. Which fairness story legitimises the transfer?
  4. Could each person meaningfully refuse or leave?
  5. Who can challenge the rule—and who judges?
