# Chapter 22: The Tally

> *A modern tally rope — mode-aware infrastructure for the social units closest to your life.*

**Part VII — Living the Unbundle** · [Index](README.md) · Prev: [Chapter 21](21-personal-stack.md) · Next: [Chapter 23](23-defensive-vocabulary.md)

## Opening scaffold

The bridal party fight, fourth analytical use. This time we ask: what tool would have helped them? Splitwise made it worse by Balanced-modifying everything. What if there were a tool that knew about the four modes?

## Structural beats

1. The bachelorette fight, fourth use.
2. The historical tally rope — a five-knight, one-rope, ten-peg coordination device that revealed preferences mechanically.
3. What modern tools (Splitwise, Venmo) get wrong (single-mode imposition).
4. The four-button design — O / V / B / I — and how the math differs per mode.
5. The visualisation — one canvas, everyone's net position, circular debts auto-net.
6. Use cases (flatshare, friend group, couple, festival squad, band).
7. Entering a Tally with someone is a relationship-defining commitment.

## Themes

- Tools that respect the four modes restore relationships that single-mode tools strip-mine.
- The rope was always the right primitive — we just had to update the materials.
- Products can carry frameworks.

## Twists

- The Tally is a real product proposal.
- The reader, who may have absorbed "this is just a thinking framework," is shown that the framework has a *thing* — a buildable artefact that completes the loop from concept to practice.

## Subarcs
