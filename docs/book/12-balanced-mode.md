# Chapter 12: Balanced Mode

> *"Are we even?" The mode of friendship, peer ledgers, and symmetric reciprocity over time.*

**Part IV — The Four Modes Up Close** · [Index](README.md) · Prev: [Chapter 11](11-value-mode.md) · Next: [Chapter 13](13-obligated-mode.md)

## Opening scaffold

Six bridesmaids fighting over a Splitwise tab three weeks before the wedding. The fight has nothing to do with money and everything to do with money.

## Structural beats

1. The Splitwise fight.
2. Definition of Balanced mode.
3. Where it lives (friendships, flatshares, peer relationships).
4. The tell: *"are we even?"*
5. Trap one — weaponised score-keeping ("I did X for you, you owe me Y").
6. Trap two — unequal capacity hidden inside equal split.
7. How Splitwise corrupts Immediate by enforcing Balanced everywhere.
8. Defence — name the ledger out loud, watch for secret scores.

## Themes

- Tools shape modes.
- The friction-reduction sold by Splitwise is also a friction-reduction in *what kind of relationship the friends are having*.
- Not all bookkeeping is benign.

## Twists

- Splitwise — generally received as a friendly-helpful app — is reframed as a Balanced-mode-imposer that quietly converts gifts into debts.
- The reader's everyday tool is, on inspection, doing political work.

## Subarcs

- Sets up Chapter 22, where the Tally (mode-aware version of Splitwise) will be proposed as the alternative.

## Closing scaffold

Balanced needs memory. The next mode runs on something else entirely — authority.
