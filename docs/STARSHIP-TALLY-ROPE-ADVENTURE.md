## 📱 **CHAPTER 5A: THE TIME INDICES**
*You chose to create indexed long-term obligations*

**The Problem**: "I owe you 10 meters of rope in 2 years" is meaningless if rope value changes.

**Kate's Innovation**: The Shipboard Survival Index (SSI) integrated with rope.

```
ROPE + SSI HYBRID SYSTEM
═══════════════════════════════════
Current Trades: Rope positions (immediate)
Long-term Contracts: SSI units (stable)

Example Contract:
"Atlas owes Vega 5 SSI units for research support,
payable in rope at future SSI/rope conversion rate"

Current Rate: 1 SSI = 20 meters rope
```

**The Breakthrough**: Rope handles spot trading and preference revelation. SSI handles long-term stability.

**Year 2**: The dual system is working perfectly!

**Phoenix**: "I use rope for daily power trades, but my maintenance contracts are in SSI units."

**Echo**: "The rope shows immediate supply/demand, SSI gives long-term planning stability."

## 🎯 **ENDING B: THE PARALLEL SYSTEMS**  
*You chose to keep rope pure with separate systems*

**The Separation Strategy**: Keep the tally rope for pure price discovery, handle other needs separately.

- **Tally Rope**: Spot trading and preference revelation
- **Credit Ledger**: Debt tracking and time-shifted obligations  
- **Authority System**: Ship safety overrides and resource allocation
- **Community Care**: Informal gift economy for relationships

**Year 3 Result**: Works well but creates boundary disputes.

**Rio**: "Should medical emergency care go through rope or community care?"
**Atlas**: "Is this trade 'market' enough for rope or 'relationship' enough for gifts?"

**The Insight**: Even with separate systems, you can't cleanly separate human exchange into pure modes. Context determines mode, but context is often ambiguous.

**LESSON LEARNED**: Parallel systems avoid forced bundling but create coordination problems. Clean separation sounds good theoretically but is messy practically.

---

## 🎯 **ENDING C: THE ROPE SUPREMACY**
*You chose to make rope the master coordination system*

**The Master System**: Everything flows through rope mechanics.

- Authority allocations shown as rope positions
- Gift giving tracked as rope transfers  
- Long-term contracts as rope commitments
- Emergency overrides as rope redistributions

**The Problem**: Making everything visible through rope positions creates social friction.

**Vega**: "I can see exactly how much rope everyone has. It makes gift-giving feel calculating."
**Orion**: "Ship authority decisions feel illegitimate when they show up as rope manipulations."

**Year 1 Result**: Efficient but feels cold and transactional.

**LESSON LEARNED**: The tally rope is perfect for revealing trade preferences, but forcing all social interactions through market-like visibility destroys non-market relationships. Some things should stay outside the ledger.

## 🎯 **THE CORE LESSON**

**The tally rope proves that minimal money can be:**
- **Mechanically simple** (rope, pegs, pulleys)
- **Informationally sophisticated** (reveals preferences, coordinates trades, tracks balances)
- **Socially neutral** (no built-in hierarchy or authority)
- **Functionally complete** (handles price discovery, ledger, coordination)

**But even this perfect minimal system spawns the other BOVI modes because human exchange is inherently multi-modal.**

The rope adventure teaches that conscious monetary design means choosing how to bundle fairness modes rather than pretending money is mode-neutral.
