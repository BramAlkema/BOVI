# The Foundations — money from first principles, stone by stone

_The rebuilt foundation beneath `CANON.md`. Where the 20 Quirks are corrections to the standard treatment, this is the **derivation** the corrections rest on: what money is, what it's worth, what a substrate must have, and where it tends — each step drop-tested, none asserted._

## What this document is (and is not)

This is **premise-axiomatisation of one school** — the relational / subjectivist / substantivist account of money — made explicit, independent, and sufficiency-checked, _with the rival schools named, not assumed away_. It is **not** a tidying of a settled field (monetary economics has no agreed Euclid; its content is contested across Austrian, Keynesian, MMT, New-Monetarist, chartalist lines). It adds **no new theorems** — every conclusion is borrowed from the giants and _assembled_. Its contribution is the assembly, the explicit premises, and the teaching.

Posture, inherited from the canon: **theoretically partisan** (strong positions on what money _is_ and how value works — that's this document) and **politically neutral** (no position on which mode or policy should win — that stays the reader's). A tool for _seeing_, micro-first, aimed at the person being taxed rather than the one at the lever-panel.

**Epistemic status of the axiom set (Stone 4):** necessity ✓ (each drop-tested), independence ✓ (pairwise), **sufficiency argued, not proven** (by elimination of the usual extras + a working minimal instance; you cannot enumerate every conceivable missing ingredient). Claims are stated at their actual strength — no tidy wrapper above, no false modesty below.

## Stone 1 — the pull

> Money is **pulled** into existence by the need for a reliable **memory of who has given and not yet received** — because giving and receiving separate, across counterparties and across time. It is not **pushed** into value by anything it is made of.

In every mode the two halves of an exchange come apart: a favour owed back later (Balanced), tribute rendered for standing (Obligated), help returned someday, a market trade whose wants don't coincide (Value). The **double-coincidence-of-wants** is just this non-coincidence at its sharpest — the _Value-mode acute case_, not the root.

_Drop-test:_ remove the pull and money's value must come from what it's made of — folk-physics essentialism, which fails for fiat, for credit, and for a valueless tally.

_Root posture:_ the first question of any money is never "what backs it" (push) but "what separation of give-and-receive does it let people bridge" (pull).

## Stone 2 — the ledger (the mechanism, derived)

The minimal object that holds "who has given and not received" is a list of net positions — a **ledger**. So what the pull reaches for is a \*ledger of who-owes-whom = **credit\***. "Money is memory" — derived, not posited.

_Drop-test:_ a pure commodity valued for its substance cannot hold who-gave-and-didn't-receive, so it leaves the pull unanswered. Only a ledger answers Stone 1.

**The Graeber landing (a result, not a patch):** the pulled-in object is credit, which is exactly what the anthropological record shows came first — debt before coin. No reconciliation needed.

**The identity (the sharp claim):** a sufficiently-divisible, symmetrically-known _good_ **is** that ledger — holdings = balances; a good moving A→B = one entry (physical conservation enforces double-entry for free); divisibility = resolution; symmetrically-known total = integrity. So commodity money was _always ledger money in costume_. There was no commodity→abstract transition — only the **substrate getting thinner** (cattle → metal → coin → note → bank-ledger → chain).

**Known ≠ fixed:** the commodity ledger only _looks_ like it needs a fixed total; that fixity is the substrate being conserved, not a requirement. Mutual credit (signed balances, zero net, elastic gross) is the same ledger with the substrate removed and the fixity dropped.

**The modes as clearing disciplines (a frame, not a stone):** a mode is a rule about _when and whether the ledger must clear_. Value clears now; Balanced nets over a window; Obligated clears on schedule, with penalty; Immediate never clears — recording it as clearable is the insult. Formal footing is borrowed: Fiske himself maps the four relational models onto the four classic measurement scales (Communal Sharing = nominal, Authority Ranking = ordinal, Equality Matching = interval, Market Pricing = ratio — Fiske 1991; 1992 _Psych. Review_), so the modes are the four **resolutions a ledger can keep**, and money is the ratio/instant-clearing discipline made infrastructural. Consistent with Stone 1 (the pull is mode-general; double-coincidence is the Value-acute case) and Stone 3's scope guard (gifts don't price at a margin). _Status:_ packaging of Fiske, no new theorem. _Drop-test:_ remove the frame and Stones 1–6 stand unchanged — it carries exposition (the money story and the mode story are one object), not load. Complements `EXTRACTION-AND-THE-MODES.md`, where the same four modes appear as extraction's _costumes_: this frame says what a mode is mechanically; that one says how extraction dresses in them.

## Stone 3 — the value: floorless but fruitful

> The ledger is worth exactly the cooperation it unlocks that couldn't happen without it — **nothing prior underneath it (floorless), real surplus inside it (fruitful)**.

_Scope (origin broad, measured value narrow):_ the origin is general across modes, but _priced_ value is trade-centric (Value/Balanced); gifts don't price at a margin, so we don't claim money's worth comes from enabling them.

**Three positions, not two:** floor (Mises/metallism — essentialism, refused) · pure bubble (Samuelson/Tirole — floorless but arbitrary) · **floorless-but-fruitful** (ours — floorless yet real, because the payoff is generated _by_ acceptance). The crux is **prior**: a floor exists before acceptance; our value exists only because the ledger is accepted — **inside the loop, not under it.**

**Two fruits:** (1) _transactional_ — surplus on trades the ledger directly mediates (Patinkin/Lagos–Wright convenience yield); (2) _option / specialisation_ — the division of labour that the **expected availability** of the ledger underwrites, accruing to people who never hold it (Smith's extent-of-the-market). The second dwarfs the first.

**Two legs:** value needs the expectation of **acceptance** (the money is good — selection) _and_ **availability** (a market is there — extent). They fail separately (good money/thin market; liquid market/bad money). They _gate_ the value; they are not the value.

**Flow, not stock:** value is realised only while the ledger circulates. A floor (store-value loaded into the medium) chokes circulation → delivers _less_ value. No-floor is therefore _functionally optimal_, not merely honest.

**Complete intersubjectivist refinement:** remove the remaining temptation to treat “social value” as one shared valuation. Acceptance is recursively intersubjective: each participant partly accepts the ledger because they expect other participants and their counterparties to accept it. The resulting price is not the sum of everyone’s beliefs and not an intrinsic social substance. It is a socially stabilised exchange ratio reflecting the holder’s expected **marginal net service**—direct trades, network access, specialisation options, and uncertainty protection—less transaction costs, monetary-layer costs, risk, and extraction. Public benefits and harms may remain outside the price unless they feed back into acceptance, availability, credibility, supply expectations, or the holder’s marginal position. Thus **intersubjectivity explains the loop; marginal surplus explains the price; power explains the distribution.**

In schematic form:

`money price ≈ PV(expected marginal monetary benefits − marginal costs − risk − extraction)`

This is a decomposition, not a claim that agents perform the calculation explicitly. “Price” remains relational: money has no absolute price apart from an exchange ratio against goods, labour, assets, or another monetary unit. Material scarcity, production, time, technology, law, and institutional capacity constrain the relation without supplying an intrinsic monetary floor.

_Drop-test (both ways):_ drop _fruitful_ → a pure bubble (can't explain selection or specialisation); drop _floorless_ → essentialism + a choked flow.

_Lineage (borrowed = strength):_ Simmel (value = relation/distance-overcome) + Patinkin (marginal utility of holding) + Lagos–Wright (microfoundation), with Samuelson/Tirole the consistency proof and the contrast.

## Stone 4 — the axioms (what a substrate must be)

Run the candidate properties through the independence test and they resolve into **two families, four requirements** — plus what the _world_ must supply.

**Family A — substrate:**

- **A1 Resolution** (_sufficiently_ divisible) — represents the entries trades need. _Drop →_ lumpy, trades throttle. Free for an abstract ledger.

**Family B — integrity (no phantom value); three independent requirements, one per attack surface:**

- **B1 Conservation** (zero-sum) — a transfer can't mint. _(A binary invariant: enforced, or the ledger is broken.)_
- **B2 Authentication** (_sufficiently_ verifiable) — a unit can't be forged.
- **B3 Symmetrically-known supply** (_sufficiently_ known) — the issuer can't dilute in secret. **Known ≠ fixed:** auditable and symmetric, not frozen; elastic supply that moves _visibly and by rule_ keeps integrity. Scarcity is only a crude, rigid over-satisfaction of B3.

_(Transferability is inherited from Stone 2 — a ledger you can't write to isn't one.)_

**The setting (world-supplied, not substrate properties):**

- **Acceptance** (selection) and **availability** (extent) — Stone 3's two legs.
- **Enforcement — the _credit form's_ residual:** why a debtor settles. A commodity ledger discharges this at the substrate (possession _is_ settlement) but pays with a **floor**; the signed-balance _credit_ form drops the floor and takes on debts that need **teeth** (reputation, recourse, collateral — Greif). _No floor, pay with teeth._ We relocate the hard problem from _what backs it_ to _why it's honoured_; we don't dissolve it.

**"Sufficiently" is a doorway, not a hedge — the skims live in the gaps.** Each spectrum requirement (A1, B2, B3) is a _threshold_; the shortfall between _sufficient_ and _perfect_ houses a characteristic skim:

| requirement        | type             | the gap below perfect houses                     |
| ------------------ | ---------------- | ------------------------------------------------ |
| Resolution         | spectrum         | rounding / minimum-denomination                  |
| Authentication     | spectrum         | counterfeiting + verification cost               |
| Known supply/terms | spectrum         | **Cantillon (supply) + spreads/opacity (terms)** |
| Conservation       | binary invariant | — no gap                                         |

Extraction follows **information asymmetry**, so it pools in the **known-ness gap** — which is why the framework's skim X-rays (`Cantillon`, `Stigler`) are known-ness detectors. The requirements guarantee _function_, not _fairness_; the gaps are the fairness battleground.

**The anti-essentialist result, as a checklist:** the substrate requirements are exactly the **ledger** properties. Scarcity, durability, intrinsic worth, backing appear in _neither family_ — everything the goldbug, cryptopoof, and nationalist point to as "what makes it real" fails to appear in the requirements at all.

## Stone 5 — the bolt-ons (the negations)

Run every "essential" that isn't an axiom through the double test — **neither necessary nor sufficient** — and sort by _why_ it fails. Three fates:

**Redundant** (crude over-satisfaction of an axiom): _scarcity_ → B3 (rare stamps aren't money) · _durability_ → B2-over-time (granite isn't money) · _portability_ → transferability (bank money never moves).

**Misplaced** (a real function, but it belongs unbundled in its own tool — Quirk 14): _store of value_ → productive assets (gold in a vault doesn't circulate) · _unit of account_ → an index/PDA (transact in pesos, account in USD) · _stability_ → the unit (a price index is stable, not a medium).

**Nothing** (essentialist fiction, does no work): _backing_ (fiat works unbacked; gold-scrip nobody accepts isn't money) · _intrinsic/use value_ (diamonds aren't money) · _the token itself_ (mutual credit has none; arcade tokens are money only inside their acceptance network — the ledger, not the token).

**This pays down the sufficiency debt:** every "you also need X" turns out redundant, misplaced, or nothing. With a **working minimal instance** (`Kocherlakota.sol` — exactly the axioms, none of the bolt-ons, functioning), sufficiency is **argued** (elimination + existence proof), though not deductively closed.

_Anti-essentialist clincher:_ gold _looked_ essential because it carried the whole bolt-on package at once (scarce _and_ durable _and_ useful _and_ a token). Strip them one at a time and the ledger is still standing, still working. The substance was always the bolt-on; the ledger was always the money.

## Stone 6 — the medium/store tradeoff (of which "melt" is one pole)

Not a prescription ("money should melt") but an **unavoidable tradeoff** in the design space. A good _store_ rewards holding (→ hoarded → bad medium); a good _medium_ rewards parting-with (→ poor hold → bad store). Holding and parting-with are opposite dispositions toward the same object, so **store-quality and medium-quality are negatively coupled in one instrument** — Quirk 14 made rigorous via the two-stroke circulation engine. You can't co-optimise; so **unbundle** (a melting medium _plus_ a separate appreciating store — nobody loses the store, it just isn't the rail).

**"Melt" = engineered not to be a hoardable store**, two mechanisms: _demurrage_ (Gesell — a cost on holding-duration, biting hoarders, sparing transactors) and _elasticity_ (known ≠ fixed — no scarcity-appreciation). The inversion of the hard-money design, which engineers _appreciation_ → store → hoarding → a bad medium (the deflation engine).

**The honesty calibration** — demurrage and the inflation tax both make held money lose value, but sit at opposite ends of the **known-ness axis**: demurrage is _known, symmetric, avoidable, rule-bound_ (a feature); the inflation tax is _hidden, asymmetric/Cantillon, non-consented_ (a skim). Same surface; opposite known-ness. Demurrage is the _honest_ version of keeping money from being hoarded.

_Honest costs:_ it's the Gesellian/relational school's design (rivals reject it); largely **untested at scale** (Wörgl is suggestive, small, shut down); and it **relocates friction onto the user** (manage the stack — Doña Elena's "exhausting" five layers).

_Drop-test:_ make the medium a fixed, appreciating store → hoarded → _willing-to-part-with-it_ collapses → it fails as a medium (Bitcoin's deflationary hoarding; Keynes's liquidity trap).

## The institutional layer — money is never studied in isolation

The stones give the **design space**; _which_ design gets built where is an **institutional/power contest**, not a technical gradient (Quirk 18 — the state is inside the model). Extraction has **two axes**:

- **impersonal ↔ directed** — the structural skim-in-a-gap (Cantillon, spreads) vs the _aimed_ lever (tariffs, devaluation, interest rates, sanctions): the **O-mode** wielding money as a weapon, **redistributive and punitive**. The reserve issuer taxes the whole world's holders; foreign holders have **zero recourse** — taxation without representation, coerced O-mode at global scale.
- **stabilising ↔ predatory** (the consent axis) — the _same_ instruments are dual-use: shock-absorption (Krugman's stabiliser) _or_ predation. The X-ray is _who bears it, did they consent, do they have recourse_ — not "all policy is theft."

_Pedigree:_ exorbitant privilege (Eichengreen), Triffin dilemma, beggar-thy-neighbour (Robinson), weaponised interdependence (Farrell & Newman), Hirschman's _Exit, Voice, and Loyalty_ (no voice + locked-in exit = maximally extractable), financial repression (Reinhart, McKinnon–Shaw), institutional economics (North, Acemoglu–Robinson, Ostrom).

**Why the schools live at the macro lever-panel:** that's where extraction is _leveraged_, and where prestige and funding sit (public choice, turned on the profession). The macro aggregate (M2, CPI) is also a **veil** — it washes out _who bears the skim_. All three rival schools fetishise _controlling the aggregate_ — goldbug _fixes_ it, cryptopoof _caps_ it, Keynesian _manages_ it — fighting over the level of a number. The micro-ledger truth spoils all three at once: the action is in **relations and distribution**, not the total's level. The framework is **micro-first and arms the extracted** (Doña Elena; personal indices over CPI — Quirk 13). _A tool for the taxed, not the taxer._

_(Calibration: Keynesians are not essentialists and do have microfoundations; the honest charge is that representative-agent macro washes out the distributional reality — HANK only lately restores it. Common thread: aggregate-veil + playbook-protection, not an identical error.)_

## The arc — money's roundabout regression

**Guard (canon: not predicting the future of money):** what follows is **forces + a comparative law**, never an outcome — no winner, no date. And it is _our school's_ reading; a rival reads the same history bending toward its pole.

- **Origin** (Stones 1–2): a minimal ledger, credit-first, near-zero extraction.
- **The detour** (where it went): two _separable_ trends — the **substrate thinned** (efficiency climbed the tradeability gradient, per `MONEY-ABIOGENESIS.md`) _while_ **extraction accreted** in the sufficiency-gaps and was then weaponised geopolitically. Money grew more capable _and_ more skimmed at once.
- **The contested field** (where it goes): **no single technical terminus.** The efficiency-pull points at a minimal/melting/unbundled/transparent ledger that _resembles the origin at higher technology_ — the "roundabout regression," a spiral landing on a **ledger, not a commodity** (inverse of Mises). But that is only **one of three engineered attractors**, selected by the power contest:
  - **Relational-minimal** (efficiency pull) — _ours_.
  - **Essentialist-hard-money** (the push) — fixed/capped/appreciating.
  - **Authoritarian-control** (the O-mode) — surveilled, centralised, programmable, _extraction-as-feature_ (China's e-CNY).
    This is **not a redemption arc**: the authoritarian pole means the arc can bend toward _more_ extraction. No guaranteed ending.

**The comparative law (the real falsifiable claim):** money regresses toward the minimal ledger **where the extraction surface is contestable** (measured _ex-ante_: exit, transparency, competition) and **persists or is captured where it's defended**. This is **conditional beta-convergence** (Barro–Sala-i-Martin) applied to the ledger; contestability is itself the _prize_ of the contest (China removes exit; weaponisation breeds exit-seeking — self-undermining, but slowly).

> **The three attractors are a _map_ of the design-space poles — a taxonomy for reading where a money sits — not a test.** With all three currently observable, "one pole won locally" is unfalsifiable, and "my pole wins globally" is the forbidden prophecy. Only the **conditional law** has falsifiable content. The map labels the poles; the law is the physics.

**The efficiency↔extraction coupling (borrowed; cf. seigniorage / rent-extraction / Cantillon / financial-repression literature):** the properties that make money efficient are tied to its extractability. Our packaging: extraction lodges in the **axiom sufficiency-gaps**, so gap-closing strips the per-unit **rate** — _but_ more-useful money grows the skim **base**. So "regression toward minimal = de-extraction" holds on the _rate_ margin, not unconditionally; net extraction turns on which margin dominates, which contestability governs.

**Use:** a **lens, not a prophecy** — point it at any money and read which attractor pulls it, which surfaces are stripped vs dug-in, and whether the relevant surface is contestable or captured.
